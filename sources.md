# Curriculum sources

updated: 2026-08-29

Wikibooks Dutch and DLI (1961) are out. They are not a 2026 beginner course.

There is **no** modern, complete A0–A1 Dutch textbook with a licence that lets you ship it in a paid Play app. CODE+, Delftse Methode, *Nederlands in gang*, Opmaat, LINK, Naar Nederland, oefenen.nl: all copyright. Free to study ≠ free to republish.

## What is actually modern and usable

### 1. Official map (not lessons)

Use this as the spine of *what* to teach. Write the lessons ourselves.

- Taalunie [NT2-leerdoelen](https://nt2-leerdoelen.org/) — current A1/A2 situations (wonen, werk, gezondheid, gemeente). Dutch + Flemish variants.
- CEFR A1 “can-do” statements.
- SLO, *Doorlopende leerlijnen Nederlands als sterke vreemde taal* (2023) — level descriptors, not dialogues.

These are government / Taalunie frameworks. Goals and examples in statutes and policy docs are usable as a map. Copying a Boom workbook into the app is not.

### 2. Open raw material (modern Dutch, not a course)

- **Tatoeba** Dutch sentences: default **CC BY 2.0 FR**, commercial reuse with attribution. Filter for short A1 lines. Audio is per-speaker licence — skip unless CC-BY/CC0; generate TTS instead. https://tatoeba.org
- **OpenTaal** word list: **CC BY 3.0** or BSD. Spelling lexicon, not a learner list with translations. https://github.com/OpenTaal/opentaal-wordlist

Useful as bricks. Not a curriculum by themselves.

### 3. Living courses — ask, do not scrape

These look like the product. None of them publish CC BY / CC BY-SA. Email first.

| Source | What it is | Catch |
|---|---|---|
| [dutchfromzero.com](https://dutchfromzero.com/) | 17 short A0–A1 lessons, English, audio, quizzes. 2026. Closest shape to Learn Dutch. | No open licence on the site. |
| [Spreektaal 1](https://www.hetbegintmettaal.nl/) (VU-NT2, 2025) | 15 spoken modules, A0–A1+, daily life (jezelf, school, computer). | © Het Begint met Taal / VU. Made for volunteers. |
| [LOWAN NT2 Startpakket](https://www.lowan.nl/vo/nt2startpakket/) (2022–) | 8 ISK themes (school, body, family, food, clothes, house…). Flashcards. | © LOWAN. For schools. |
| [NederPath](https://github.com/CameronNel/nederpath) | JSON: 120 grammar rules A0–C1, 641 authored sentences, A1 readings. | No LICENSE file. Competing app. |
| [HayrullahC/inburgering](https://github.com/HayrullahC/inburgering) | JSON vocab + 72 grammar lessons, exam-shaped. | Starts at **A2**. No LICENSE file. |

A yes from dutchfromzero or VU would beat hunting PDFs.

## Do not use

Wikibooks Dutch, FSI/DLI tapes, Hasendonck 1823, Elffers 1896. Old or unreadable.

Pirate dumps on Internet Archive (Dummies, Teach Yourself, CODE+, Colloquial). Still in copyright.

NT2Lex: CEFR word list but **CC BY-NC-SA** — IAP is commercial, out.

Sites with no licence (dutchgrammar.com, NT2 TaalMenu, Juf M YouTube): copyright by default.

## Decision

**Do not import a mummy PDF.** Two real options:

1. **Write the course.** NT2-leerdoelen + CEFR A1 as the syllabus. Original dialogues/vocab in 2026 Dutch. Tatoeba/OpenTaal as extra sentences/words. TTS at build. This is original content; Play-safe; matches “AI makes the conversion cheap.”
2. **Licence a living course.** First ask: dutchfromzero, then Spreektaal/VU.

Until a licence lands, (1) is the spine.
